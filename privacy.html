Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

Your music never reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go. The vocal remover runs entirely inside your own browser, on your own computer. We have no account system, no database and no server that receives audio.

Your audio

When you press Start, your browser asks which tab to share and hands that tab's sound to the page. That sound is processed on your machine and played back through a small window. It is never uploaded, never recorded and never written to a file. When you press Stop, or close the window, it is gone.

What is stored on your device

These stay in your browser. They are never sent anywhere, and clearing your site data removes them.

  • Your settings (local storage, bgm.settings): the remover quality, the strength slider and your Sound choices, so the page remembers them next time.
  • The AI model (cache storage, about 56 MB): downloaded once so you do not fetch it again.
  • An offline copy of the page (cache storage), so it still opens without a connection.

What the page loads from elsewhere

  • The AI model file, from Hugging Face, the first time you choose AI mode. That request tells them your IP address, as any download does.
  • The code that runs the model (ONNX Runtime), from the jsDelivr network.

Nothing about what you listen to is included in either request.

Analytics

There are none. No tracking script, no visitor counter, no fingerprinting.

Advertising

This site may show ads supplied by Google AdSense to cover its costs. If and when it does, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and read how Google uses data at its partner-sites page. Ads are never shown inside the remover itself, and they receive no audio: they cannot, because the audio never leaves your browser.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The full history is public in the source repository.
