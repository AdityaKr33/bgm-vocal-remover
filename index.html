Take the vocals out of any tab, while it plays

Point it at a music tab and the singing goes, live. An AI model runs on your own graphics card, so nothing is uploaded, nothing is recorded, and there is no account to make.

  • Any site. YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, a radio stream.

Live vocal remover

  1. Open the music you want in another tab.
  2. Press Start and pick that tab. Tick Also share tab audio.
  3. A small window opens and plays it back without the vocals.

How it works

It listens to a tab, not to a file

Your browser can hand one tab's sound to another page. That is what the Start button asks for. There is no upload and no waiting for a file to process: the music is cleaned as it plays, a second or so behind.

The AI remover

A trained model (MDX-Net Inst HQ 5) splits the music from the voice. It runs on your graphics card through WebGPU. The model is 56 MB and downloads once, then stays in your browser. The voice drops to about 2 % of its level.

The Fast remover

No graphics card needed. Singers are mixed dead centre, so this cancels whatever sits in the middle of the stereo picture, while protecting the bass and keeping drum hits. It is gentler, with almost no delay.

Then you shape it

Bass, Warmth, Instruments, Clarity, Treble and stereo width, with twelve ready-made styles from Deep focus to Late night. A limiter keeps the level safe whatever you choose.

What it works with

Anything that plays sound in a browser tab. There is no list to be on and no integration to set up, because it listens to the tab rather than to a service.

  • YouTube
  • YouTube Music
  • SoundCloud
  • Bandcamp
  • Internet radio
  • Apple Music on the web
  • Deezer
  • Your own files in a tab

It needs Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer. Safari and Firefox cannot share a tab's sound yet, and nor can phones or tablets.

Nothing is uploaded

Most vocal removers make you upload a file to their servers. This one does not have servers to upload to. The AI model is fetched once, like an image on a web page, and then every bit of the work happens inside your own browser on your own machine. Your music is never sent anywhere, never stored and never recorded. The full privacy policy says so in detail.

Questions

Is my music uploaded anywhere?
No. The sound never leaves your computer. The model runs on your own graphics card, so there is no server to send audio to.
Which browsers does it work in?
Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer. Safari and Firefox cannot share a tab's sound, which this needs, and nor can phones or tablets.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no account and no payment. Open the page, pick a tab, press Start.
Why is there a short delay?
The AI model works on about six seconds of sound at a time, so the cleaned sound runs roughly 1.3 to 1.5 seconds behind on a recent laptop GPU. The Fast remover has almost no delay.
How well does it remove the vocals?
With the AI remover the voice drops to about 2 % of its level while the music stays. The Fast remover is lighter: it reduces a centred voice but leaves more of it behind.
Why does a second window open?
If this page played the cleaned sound itself, the share would capture that sound again and you would hear a loop. The small window has its own audio output, outside the share.
Can I use it for karaoke or practice?
Yes, that is what it is for: backing tracks to sing or play over, and music to study to without the lyrics pulling your attention.
